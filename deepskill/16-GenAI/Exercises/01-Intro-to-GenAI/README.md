## What is Generative AI?

Generative AI refers to AI models that can create new content — text, images, code, music, video — rather than just analyzing or classifying existing data. These models learn patterns from massive training datasets and use that knowledge to generate original outputs.

## How Do LLMs Work?

1. **Training:** The model is trained on billions of text documents from the internet.
2. **Tokenization:** Text is broken into tokens (words or subwords).
3. **Prediction:** Given a sequence of tokens, the model predicts the next most likely token.
4. **Generation:** By repeatedly predicting the next token, the model generates coherent text.

## LLM Parameters

- **Parameters:** The "weights" of the neural network. GPT-3 has 175 billion parameters.
- **Context window:** How much text the model can remember (e.g., 8K, 32K, 128K tokens).
- **Temperature:** Controls randomness — higher = more creative, lower = more focused.
