# 16 - Generative AI

## What is Generative AI?

Generative AI refers to artificial intelligence models that can create new content — text, images, code, music, and more. Unlike traditional AI that classifies or predicts, generative AI produces original outputs. Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4 are the most popular examples.

## Why Learn Generative AI?

- **Boost productivity** — Automate content creation, code generation, and research.
- **Enhance creativity** — Get ideas, drafts, and inspiration from AI.
- **Build smarter apps** — Integrate AI capabilities into your applications.
- **Stay relevant** — GenAI is transforming the software industry.
