# 14 - Agile Methodology

## What is Agile?

Agile is an iterative approach to software development that focuses on delivering value to customers quickly and adapting to change. Instead of planning everything upfront, Agile teams work in short cycles called sprints and continuously improve.

## Why Learn Agile?

- **Respond to change** — Requirements evolve, and Agile embraces that.
- **Faster delivery** — Working software is delivered in small increments.
- **Customer involvement** — Regular feedback ensures the product meets real needs.
- **Team collaboration** — Self-organizing teams work closely together.
