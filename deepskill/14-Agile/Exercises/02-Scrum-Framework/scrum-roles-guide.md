# Scrum Framework — Roles, Artifacts & Events

## Scrum Roles

### Product Owner
- Defines the product vision and strategy
- Creates and manages the Product Backlog
- Prioritizes backlog items based on business value
- Acts as the voice of the customer

### Scrum Master
- Facilitates all Scrum events
- Coaches the team on Agile practices
- Removes impediments blocking the team
- Shields the team from external distractions

### Development Team
- Self-organizing (no one assigns tasks)
- Cross-functional (all skills needed to deliver)
- Typically 3-9 members
- Collectively accountable for delivery

## Scrum Artifacts

### Product Backlog
- Living document of all desired features
- Items written as user stories
- Prioritized by the Product Owner
- Never complete — continuously refined

### Sprint Backlog
- Selected items for the current sprint
- Includes a plan for delivering them
- Owned by the Development Team
- Updated daily as work progresses

### Increment
- Sum of all completed backlog items in a sprint
- Must meet the Definition of Done
- Potentially releasable to users

## Scrum Events

| Event | Duration | Purpose |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Sprint Planning | 2-4 hours | Select backlog items for the sprint |
| Daily Scrum | 15 min | Sync, plan next 24 hours |
| Sprint Review | 1-2 hours | Demo completed work |
| Sprint Retrospective | 1-1.5 hours | Improve processes |
